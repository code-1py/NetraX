Legal and safe use
==================

NetraX is released under the MIT License. It invokes Nmap as an external
subprocess and does not include Nmap. Nmap is distributed under its own
license; review the `Nmap license <https://nmap.org/book/man-legal.html>`_.

You are responsible for complying with applicable laws and the terms that
govern the systems you scan. Scan only networks and systems you own or have
explicit permission to test.
